To get the source IP address we did:
source="ssh.log"
| rex field=_raw "^\S+\s+\S+\s+(?<src_ip>\d{1,3}(?:\.\d{1,3}){3})" |stats count by src_ip
The top ip address with 2380 counts found: 
src_ip = 192.168.202.141

What our regex means:
^ start at the beginning 
\S+ skip the first column 
\s+ skip the white spaces
These are done twice
() - capturing group 
?<src_Ip>.. - what to name the capture group
\d{1,3}(?:\.\d{1,3}){3} -get IP address in IPv4 form (quick and loose verision)
